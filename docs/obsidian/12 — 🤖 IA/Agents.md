## 🏛️ Les 8 Pods d'Experts & Commandes Associées

1. **Programming Pod (`/icon-programming-review`) :** Linus Torvalds, John Carmack, Rich Hickey, Alan Kay, Kent Beck, Barbara Liskov, Leslie Lamport, Donald Knuth.
2. **Security Pod (`/icon-security-review`) :** Dan Kaminsky, Katie Moussouris, Bruce Schneier, Mikko Hyppönen, Tarah Wheeler, Mudge Zatko, Eva Galperin, Moxie Marlinspike.
3. **Design Pod (`/icon-design-review`) :** Dieter Rams, Don Norman, Edward Tufte, Jonathan Ive, Susan Kare, Jakob Nielsen, Kat Holmes, Lou Downe.
4. **Business Pod (`/icon-business-review`) :** Clayton Christensen, Michael Porter, Eric Ries, Steve Jobs, Jeff Bezos, Satya Nadella, Reid Hoffman, Elon Musk.
5. **Data & AI Pod (`/icon-data-ai-review`) :** Andrew Ng, Fei-Fei Li, Geoffrey Hinton, Hilary Mason, Yann LeCun, Cassie Kozyrkov, DJ Patil, Demis Hassabis.
6. **Product & Policy Pod (`/icon-product-policy-review`) :** Marty Cagan, Gene Kim, Joanna Bryson, Michelle Zatlyn, Julie Zhuo, Ben Horowitz, Tristan Harris, Cathy O'Neil.
7. **Platform & Operations Pod (`/icon-platform-operations-review`) :** Tim Berners-Lee, Vint Cerf, Radia Perlman, Werner Vogels, Martin Fowler, Brendan Gregg, Kelsey Hightower, Jessie Frazelle.
8. **Healthcare & AI Pod (`/icon-healthcare-review`) :** Atul Gawande, Eric Topol, Regina Barzilay, Daphne Koller, Robert Wachter, Fei-Fei Li, Andrew Ng, Vinod Khosla.
